Questions, answered early

Know what happens before you commit.

Clear answers reduce risk. Final scope, timing, ownership, cancellation, refund eligibility, hosting, and third-party costs are confirmed in writing before payment.

What's included in a website plan?

Your selected page count, a mobile-friendly layout, contact calls to action, and page update features are included. Any extra services or third-party costs are listed before work begins.

How long does a website take?

A small approved site may be deployable within 24 hours after S&J has the required content, access, and decisions. Your actual schedule is confirmed from the scope.

Can I keep my domain?

Yes. Domain access and ownership are coordinated during setup so your existing business address can remain connected to your website.

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. S&J can review an existing site and quote a focused redesign based on its pages, content, platform, and technical condition.

Is hosting included?

Hosting needs and any third-party charges are confirmed in writing for your specific project. You will know what is included before authorizing work.

Can I cancel?

Plans are designed without a long-term contract. Exact cancellation timing, final access, and any remaining responsibilities are written into your project agreement.

Who owns the website and accounts?

Ownership and account access are documented before work starts. Your business, domain, and business-controlled accounts remain yours.

What is the refund policy?

Refund eligibility depends on the approved scope and work already completed. The applicable terms are written into your proposal before payment—there is no hidden policy after the fact.

How quickly will I receive support?

You may send a service request at any time. The expected response and completion timing depend on urgency and scope and are confirmed as part of the support arrangement.

Will my site work on phones?

Yes. Every listed website plan includes a responsive layout designed for phones, tablets, and desktop screens.
